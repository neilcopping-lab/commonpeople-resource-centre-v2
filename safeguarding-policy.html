Safeguarding policy.

How we keep children and young people safe, who to contact, and exactly what happens when someone raises a concern.

Need help right now? If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. Young people can talk to Childline free on 0800 1111. To raise a worry about Comquat, see our Keeping you safe page.

Our commitment

Comquat helps people of all ages into work, and many of them are young people building their first CV. Every child and young person who uses Comquat has the right to be safe. We build our tools so the safe way is the easy way, and we act quickly and properly if we're ever worried about someone.

Who this covers. Everyone who uses Comquat (CV Rewrite, Interview Prep, the CV Builder and coaching calls), and anyone who works on Comquat for Comquat Ventures UK Ltd, now or in future. In this policy, a child is anyone under 18.

Safeguarding lead. Neil Copping, founder, Comquat Ventures UK Ltd. Email: neil.copping@the-common-people.com (subject line "Safeguarding concern").

The safeguarding lead reads every concern personally, replies within one working day, decides what action to take and keeps a record. If Comquat grows to include anyone else, a deputy will be named here.

When a concern reaches us

A concern might come in by email, during a coaching call, through something someone writes or says in Comquat, or from a parent, teacher or employer.

  1. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999 straight away. Hertfordshire's advice is to call the police on 999 if a child is in immediate danger, left alone or missing (North Herts Council).
  2. Listen, and don't promise to keep it secret. Tell the young person you'll only share it with people who can help. Don't ask leading questions or try to investigate.
  3. Write it down the same day. What was said, in the person's own words where possible, the date and time, and what you did.
  4. Decide what to do, the same day.
    • A child may be at risk of harm, and lives in Hertfordshire: call Hertfordshire County Council Children's Services on 0300 123 4043, which takes concerns about a child under 18, including out of hours (North Herts Council).
    • The child lives elsewhere: contact the children's services where the child lives (East and North Herts NHS Trust).
    • Not sure whether it's serious enough: ask the NSPCC for advice on 0808 800 5000 (Hertsmere Council).
    • A worry that isn't about harm (for example, someone feeling low about job hunting): point them to the right help, such as Childline on 0800 1111 for young people (Hertsmere Council).
  5. Tell the person who raised it what we've done, unless that would put someone at greater risk.
  6. Never pass on details for any other reason. Safeguarding information is shared only with the people who need it to keep someone safe.

If the concern is about the safeguarding lead, contact Hertfordshire Children's Services directly on the same number.

How Comquat keeps young people safe

By design

  • Comquat is for people aged 16 and over, confirmed at sign-up.
  • No messaging, comments or public profiles, so nobody can contact a young person through Comquat.
  • Accounts are private. We only look inside one when the person asks for help, or to fix a problem.
  • Emails start unticked. No adverts, no tracking, nothing sold.
  • Names and contact details are swapped for placeholders before anything goes to the AI. The AI's follow-up questions never ask about health, family circumstances or the reason for a career break.
  • Practice videos stay on the person's own device. The video step tells everyone to upload as Unlisted, keep uniform, street signs and house numbers out of shot, only send links through the employer, and, if under 18, watch it with a trusted adult first.
  • Under-18s are told to leave a photo off their CV.
  • "Need help?" and "Report a concern" links sit on every builder page, the account page and the sign-up page, and lead to the Keeping you safe page.

Coaching calls with under-18s

  • Under-18s are asked to tell a parent or carer before booking, and the parent or carer is welcome to sit in.
  • Calls are by video or phone only, never in person, and are not recorded.
  • Calls stay on job hunting: CVs, interviews and next steps. No contact on personal social media, before or after.
  • Any concern that comes up on a call follows the procedure above.

Anything new. Before Comquat adds anything that lets other people see or contact a user (for example, profiles employers can view), the safeguarding lead reviews this policy and our data protection impact assessment first.

Records, training and review

Records. Each concern is written up in a private safeguarding log kept by the safeguarding lead, separate from Comquat accounts: date, what was raised, what was decided, who was contacted and why. The log is shared only with the services named in the procedure, and only when needed to keep someone safe.

Training. The safeguarding lead completes an introductory safeguarding course and refreshes it every year. Anyone who later works on Comquat reads this policy before they start.

Review. This policy is reviewed every year, after any serious concern, and before any change that lets people see or contact Comquat users. Next review: 20 November 2027.
