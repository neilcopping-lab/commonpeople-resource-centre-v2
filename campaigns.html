Campaign · #StopGhostingJobSeekers

Stop ghosting job seekers.

65% of UK job seekers have been ghosted by a recruiter, and 86% felt down or depressed afterwards (Tribepad, survey of 2,000 UK job seekers). The ask is tiny: one brief message to everyone who applied.

Campaign · #PayTheFirstYear

Pay the first year.

Use the estimated £700 million of levy money that never reaches apprenticeships (FE Week) to pay the full first-year wage for 16 to 24 year olds taken on by small and medium sized businesses. Start with a pilot here in Hertfordshire.
