To upload (about 2 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Go to github.com/neilcopping-lab/commonpeople-resource-centre-v2 and sign in.
3. Click "Add file", then "Upload files".
4. Drag in the 9 .html files AND the img folder (drag the folder itself,
   so the pictures land in img/). Leave this HOW-TO file out.
5. Commit directly to main.
Cloudflare Pages updates the live site within a few minutes.
