What a sponsor gets, each edition

  • A clearly-labelled sponsor block in the edition: your logo, two or three lines, and one link.

  • Exclusivity: one sponsor per edition, so you are the only brand in the room.

Every placement is labelled honestly, in the spirit of the site: "Supported by [you]. They paid to keep the Loudspeaker free. They had no say in a word of it." That line is the point, it makes your support a mark of trust rather than an interruption.
