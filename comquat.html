Comquat for Job Seekers · Free

Free tools to get you hired.

Comquat for Job Seekers is the free toolkit from The Com'mon People for working out what's next, getting your CV right and nailing the interview. It's built by someone who spent nine years in recruitment and then found himself on the other side of the desk. No card, no credits, no catch.

Five tools, one free account

Honest by design
