Keeping young people's data safe.

A plain-English summary of our risk check: what could go wrong for young people using Comquat, and what we do about it.

What this is

Before we let young people use Comquat, we wrote a data protection impact assessment, or DPIA. It's a written check of the risks our tools create for young people, and what we do about each one. The Information Commissioner's Office's Children's code expects one from any online service young people are likely to use. Our Create your 1st CV journey is built for school and college students, so we need one.

It covers CV Rewrite, Interview Prep, the CV Builder, the pitch video and Comquat accounts, all run by Comquat Ventures UK Ltd. Beyond the Obvious, added in November 2026, has its own addendum, which looks at how it keeps 16 and 17 year olds' answers and any health details safe.

What we found

With our safeguards in place, no risk to young people is left at high. Three are left at medium. That's mostly because they depend on what happens once something leaves Comquat, such as a video someone uploads elsewhere. We've explained each one below.

Who can use Comquat

Comquat is for people aged 16 and over. You confirm you're 16 or older when you sign up, and our server won't create an account without that tick. In the UK, children aged 13 and over can agree to things like emails for themselves (ICO). We've set our minimum higher than that, at 16.

We don't ask for your exact age or date of birth. Instead, everyone gets the same safe settings.

What we never collect

  • Your date of birth or exact age
  • Your home location, or any location tracking
  • Cookies for tracking or adverts

Nothing is sold, and the AI services we use don't use your information to train their models.

Newer features

We checked the job search profile, profile photo, monthly check-in email, Share Comquat and suggested job roles when they went live on 20 November 2026. None of them shares anything about you with anyone else. Profile photos are only seen by you, and have their location details removed. Check-in emails are off unless you switch them on, and every one has a one-tap Stop.

We also tested the CV Builder's AI for bias. It was writing age and dyslexia into personal statements, so we fixed it: write-ups now leave out age, health, religion, origin and family details. That test now runs every month and after every change.

Before anything changes

We'll do a fresh risk check before adding anything that lets other people see or contact you, such as a profile employers can view, any messaging, or a photo on a profile that others can see.

Who checked it

The assessment was written on 20 November 2026 with AI help, checked against the live tools, and approved by Neil Copping, founder of Comquat Ventures UK Ltd. It hasn't yet been reviewed by an independent data protection expert. It's reviewed every year, and before any of the changes above.

Questions
