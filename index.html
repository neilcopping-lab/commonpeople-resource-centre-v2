Introducing Comquat

Comquat is the family of apps from The Com'mon People. Three free tools for anyone looking for work: Interview Prep, CV Rewrite and the CV Builder, all on one free account. And Comquat for Recruiters, coming soon, for the people doing the hiring.

No card, no credits, no catch for job seekers. AI writes the first draft, a human always checks it, and nothing is ever invented.

Why I built it

I spent nine years in recruitment, then I was made redundant and found myself on the other side of the desk.

I know what makes a recruiter stop and read, and I know most candidates are never told. So I built the tools I'd want in your shoes: help to get your CV and your interview right for the one job you really want. Two good applications beat fifty rushed ones.

For recruiters, I'm building the tool that turns a good interview into a profile a client will actually read, checked by the recruiter before anyone sees it, so the real person comes across.

Neil Copping, The Com'mon People
