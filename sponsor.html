Sponsor The Loudspeaker.

One clearly-labelled sponsor per edition. No ad clutter, no pop-ups, no selling anyone's data. A single, honest introduction to people on both sides of hiring, and it is what keeps the whole resource free.

Who reads it

1,100 subscribers and growing, in a tight professional niche: HR, talent and recruitment people, marketing, finance, events and operations, plus the jobseekers they are trying to reach. In other words, exactly the audience that training providers, HR software, recruiters and coaching firms pay to reach, and rarely reach well.

The Loudspeaker goes out monthly as a LinkedIn newsletter and lives on as a full web edition here, so a single placement keeps working long after the send.

What a sponsor gets, each edition

  • A clearly-labelled sponsor block in the edition: your logo, two or three lines, and one link.
  • An evergreen "Supported by" credit on that edition's web page, which keeps earning as an archive.
  • Exclusivity: one sponsor per edition, so you are the only brand in the room.

Every placement is labelled honestly, in the spirit of the site: "Supported by [you]. They paid to keep the Loudspeaker free. They had no say in a word of it." That line is the point: it makes your support a mark of trust rather than an interruption.
