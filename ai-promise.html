Our AI promise.

Comquat uses AI. We're not going to pretend otherwise. But we use it the way a good recruiter would: to save you time, never to replace you. Here's exactly what that means.

1. You're in charge of how much AI you use

Every account starts on Essentials only. AI does the main job of each tool and nothing more. You can change this any time in My account:

  • Full help adds extras, like suggested job roles and help with your intro video script
  • Essentials only means AI does the core job, nothing extra
  • Off means no AI at all. We'll point you to our free guides, templates and checklists instead

Turning AI down or off never locks you out of your account, your profile or anything you've saved.

2. A human at every gate

Nothing AI writes leaves Comquat until a person has checked it, and that person is you. Before you download a CV, you tick a box to confirm it's true and nothing has been made up. If AI got something wrong, you'll spot it before anyone else sees it.

3. What AI does here

  • Tailors your CV to a job, using only what you've told us
  • Builds your interview prep report and researches the company, with every source linked
  • Turns your answers into a CV draft in the CV Builder

4. What AI never does

  • Never sees your name, contact details or photos. We strip them out first.
  • Never makes decisions about you
  • Never ranks you, scores you or rejects you
  • Never shares anything with an employer. Your profile is private unless you choose otherwise.
  • Never sends anything on your behalf

5. Honest about mistakes

AI can get things wrong, so everything it writes is labelled. If it adds something you didn't say, take it out. Your CV should be something you can stand behind in every interview.

6. Honesty checks, built in

AI sometimes tries to make things sound better than they were. So before you see your CV, it's checked against what you told us. Anything that can't be backed up by your own words gets flagged or taken out. We check:

  • Job titles, employers and dates
  • Qualifications and grades
  • Numbers and achievements
  • Skills and tools
  • How much you were responsible for

Check it again, any time

When you've tailored a CV in CV Rewrite, press the Honesty check button and we'll run a fresh check. Anything that doesn't match what you've told us gets highlighted, so you can fix it or explain it. You decide what stays.

7. Fair for everyone

We test the CV Builder's AI for bias every month and every time we change how it works. If we find a problem, we fix it.

8. Your data stays safe

Files you upload are deleted from our server as soon as we've pulled the text out. The AI services we use don't use your information to train their models. Full details are in our Privacy notice.

9. If you're 16 or 17

The same promise applies, with extra care. Read more on our Safeguarding page.

10. For recruiters using Comquat for Recruiters

Agencies get the same choice. Each agency picks its setting in Settings, and every agency starts on Essentials only:

  • Full help adds the draft introduction email to the client, the draft feedback email to the candidate, interview questions matched to the job spec, and a tidy of the candidate's CV under the agency's branding
  • Essentials only means AI does the core job: the interview transcript, the job spec and skills match, the client brief, the candidate profile and the highlights picks
  • Off means no AI at all. Recruiters can still book interviews, record them, collect e-signatures and send profiles they've written themselves

The same human gates apply. A recruiter checks every profile and signs a declaration that it's a true reflection of the person they interviewed. The candidate checks it too, and chooses whether a client sees their full highlights video, hears their voice only, or gets no video at all. The AI suggests how a candidate matches the job spec for the recruiter to check, but it never decides who gets put forward. People do that. We also test the recruiter profiles for bias every month and after every change to how the AI works.

11. Talk to us

Got a worry, or think the AI got something wrong? Email customer.service@the-common-people.com and a real person will reply. For anything about your data, email privacy@the-common-people.com.
