COMQUAT ROLL-OUT: THREE UPLOADS

Do them in this order. Each one is the same drag-and-drop as before.

1. THE WEBSITE
   Open: github.com/neilcopping-lab/commonpeople-resource-centre-v2/upload/main
   Drag EVERYTHING inside the "1-SITE-upload" folder (the four files and the img folder) onto the page.
   Click "Commit changes". The site updates in a minute or two.

2. THE CV REWRITE APP (cv.the-common-people.com)
   Open: github.com/neilcopping-lab/cv-rewrite-app/upload/main
   Drag the "public" folder from inside "2-CV-APP-upload" onto the page.
   Click "Commit changes". Render rebuilds the app in a few minutes.

3. THE INTERVIEW PREP APP
   Open: github.com/neilcopping-lab/interview-prep-app-v2/upload/main
   Drag the "public" folder from inside "3-PREP-APP-upload" onto the page.
   Click "Commit changes". Render rebuilds the app in a few minutes.
